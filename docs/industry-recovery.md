# Industry recovery

Industry is an explicit enrichment requirement. Four-pass extraction keeps up to 2,000 characters of event-scoped visible prose, excluding navigation, sponsors and multi-event catalogs. The service-only preview uses the live vertical registry before any optional research.

A missing industry keeps the existing Crawl4AI/SearXNG recovery path eligible even when region and format are known. The existing 90-second, three-page, two-search budget is shared with industry recovery. Rules are retried on the new evidence before Manus is queued. Existing provider configuration and the daily credit gate remain authoritative; OpenAI is not enabled by this change.

Manus research includes directly stated industry/topic phrases. Every phrase still needs a visible organizer quote, matching event heading, same-owner scope and a fresh Crawl4AI recheck before ingestion uses it. Categories are retained; verified research has its own industry_research field to avoid conflicting with existing tags. Missing optional categories are also eligible for the extractive mini pass.

Research schema version 2 separates prior geography-only jobs. One existing queued/running/completed/failed/unknown job per source and calendar month is reused. Failed or uncertain submissions do not spawn another job automatically. Completed results cannot silently be treated as running results, or vice versa.

The database classifier persists evidence and preserves assigned verticals. Existing text is weighted strongly enough to resolve explicit descriptions; generic AI wording has less weight than an explicit industry. Ties and absent evidence still remain unresolved. This does not make a university calendar listing into a conference, publish records, or archive the remaining review queue.
